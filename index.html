Our solutions

Two domains, one mission: innovation with impact

We combine technology, didactics and user-friendly design into solutions that work in the classroom, in training and in industry.

Robotics

Robot simulation: program, test and learn – without hardware

Our robotics solutions let students, teachers and engineers program industrial and educational robots in a realistic virtual environment, straight from the browser. Experiment safely, make mistakes freely and only move to real hardware when you're ready.

  • Realistic 3D simulationRobot arms, grippers, conveyors and workcells that behave like the real thing.
  • Learn to program step by stepFrom visual blocks for beginners to real code for advanced users.
  • Ready for the classroomAssignments, challenges and progress tracking for teachers and trainers.
Education

Digital learning tools that put the student first

We develop educational tools that make learning more natural and inclusive. Our flagship approach lets students write mathematics digitally just as they would on paper – with a pen – while the software turns it into clear, structured and readable notation.

  • Natural handwriting inputWrite formulas, fractions and equations by hand on a tablet or laptop.
  • Inclusive by designSupports learners with dyslexia, dyspraxia or other learning difficulties.
  • Clean, readable resultsNeat digital work that's easy for teachers to read, review and give feedback on.
